Dear Valued Patients,

For many years, it has been our privilege to care for you and your families at Gilbart Dental Care of Frederick. The relationships we have built with our patients and community mean the world to us, and we are incredibly grateful for the trust you have placed in our team over the years.

Today, we are excited to share some wonderful news about the future of the practice. After serving as a dedicated associate doctor at our office for the past 10 years, Dr. Sana Butt has officially taken ownership of the practice from Dr. Jonathan and Dr. Lucy. Dr. Sana has become a familiar and trusted part of our dental family, and we could not be more confident in her leadership and commitment to continuing the same exceptional care our patients have always known.

Most importantly, we want to reassure you that while ownership is transitioning, the heart of the practice remains the same. You will continue to see the same doctors and the same friendly team members you already know and trust. Dr. Lucy will continue practicing in the office, and our commitment to personalized, compassionate dental care will remain unchanged. The only thing changing is our name. Gilbart Dental Care of Frederick will now be known as Mint & Molar Dental Care.

> Gilbart Dental Care of Frederick will now be known as **Mint & Molar Dental Care**.

While our name is new, our mission, values, and dedication to our patients remain exactly as they have always been. We are proud to continue serving the Frederick community with the same trusted care, familiar faces, and welcoming environment you have come to expect.

Thank you for allowing us to be part of your lives and your healthcare journey. We are excited for this next chapter and look forward to continuing to care for you and your family for many years to come.

Dr. Sana, Dr. Lucy, Dr. Jonathan<br>
and the Team at Mint &amp; Molar Dental Care
